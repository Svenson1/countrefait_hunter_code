# Banknote reference images

`ugx-1000.jpg` to `ugx-50000.jpg` show the front of each note of the Bank of
Uganda 2010 series, stamped SPECIMEN. The app shows them on the home screen and
the note step, and uses them to draw the demo sample photos and the two example
checks in History.

## Licence

These images come from LeftoverCurrency.com and carry its watermark. They are
**not** covered by the MIT licence of the code.

Before making the repository or the GitHub Pages site public, either get
permission from LeftoverCurrency.com, or replace the images with ones the team
has the rights to, such as your own photos of the notes.

## Replacing them

Keep the same file names. The app expects a landscape image of the front of the
note, about 560 × 270 pixels, as JPEG. If an image is missing, the app still
works, but that note shows no picture and the Demo button can't make sample
photos of it.
